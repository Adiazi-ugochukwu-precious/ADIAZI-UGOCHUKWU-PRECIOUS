01 / About

I build whole products, from the ledger up

I'm the co-founder and CTO of NePay, a Nigerian wallet built by Circle Technology Limited, and I lead its backend engineering. I'm also in my final year of Systems Engineering at the University of Lagos, graduating in September 2027. Before NePay, I led backend engineering at Grup Limited.

At NePay I designed and built the entire backend as one NestJS and TypeScript service on PostgreSQL: signup, two factor login and KYC; wallets on an append-only ledger; naira, crypto and US dollar deposits; bank payouts; airtime, bills and gift cards; referrals and points; live support chat; and the admin panel our operations team runs the business from. Every provider sits behind its own adapter, so we can switch one without touching the rest.

I work spec-first. Invariants get written down before code, every route is secure by default, and nothing is ‘done’ until an automated check says so. Outside fintech I've shipped Spring Boot services, real time commerce tools and smart home integrations, and the thread through all of it is the same: systems that stay correct when providers misbehave, networks drop and requests arrive twice.
